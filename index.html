About

Bridging Science, Technology, and Student Success

I began my career at Oregon State University's Food Safety and Environmental Stewardship Program (FSES), working across laboratory operations, chromatography and mass spectrometry, regulatory compliance, and laboratory information systems.

Today, I am pursuing a Master of Engineering in Computer Science, specializing in Cybersecurity with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. My graduate research examines security vulnerabilities in AI-attributed code, bringing an empirical research perspective to questions of software reliability.

Beyond my technical work, I serve as Coordinator of Student Outreach & Scholarships for Oregon State University's College of Agricultural Sciences. I oversee scholarships, advise the MANRRS and SACNAS chapters, and develop initiatives that strengthen student engagement and leadership. Through intentional outreach and programming, I helped increase chapter membership by more than 170%.
